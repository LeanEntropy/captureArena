LAND CAPTURE
CLAIM · HOLD · DOMINATE
HOW TO PLAY
  • Move with WASD / arrow keys or the mouse. Mouse wheel zooms.
  • Leave your territory to draw a trail. Close the loop back to your colour to capture the enclosed land.
  • If anyone crosses your trail, you die. Standing on enemy land while not trailing also kills you.
  • Die → respawn 3s later in your faction's territory.
  • Endangered: faction below 10% territory — when one of its players dies, they get reassigned to another team.
  • Eliminated: faction wiped out — its remaining players are reassigned to other factions.
  • Leaderboard tracks personal score (kills + claims) — your score follows you across team changes.
  • 15-min rounds. Faction with most territory wins. Highest personal score wins bragging rights.
