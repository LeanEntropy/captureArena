Visual Direction Plan Round 3 + F

Six art directions with per-direction FX, notifications, banners, countdown, and game-win moments. New: F — Atoll Hybrid (E base + D moments + A notifications) per Director request after the previous Slice A revert.

What's new — Direction F. Director's hybrid: take E's island/water world, replace the dome with a flat cylinder at a Y clearly distinct from the territory plane (the dome+territory at-the-same-Y caused Z-fighting in the previous Slice A and was reverted), use D's voxel kill/respawn/countdown/banner/win FX for chunkier moment-to-moment feedback, and use A's cream notification card style — anchored below the leaderboard so it never overlaps the existing UI (timer top-left, factions top-right, leaderboard top-right under factions, minimap bottom-left, player stats bottom-center).

What's new in round 3. Director: "at least one suggestion should be of the background as water or sea and the arena as an island." Added Direction E — Castaway Atoll. Animated procedural water (vertex sin waves + foam ring + sun-glitter, ~0.4ms/frame), grass-on-sand cylindrical island with cube cliff-rocks rim, distant atolls in the haze, a sailor-cap on each character, sundial countdown, and a victory flag that rises on a wooden pole at game-end.

What's new in round 2 (kept). Director feedback: the four directions felt too similar — same trigger claim, only 2 distinct kill FX. Round 2 fixed that:

Per-direction claim FX: A sunburst rays · B shockwave + strobe · C ink-bleed splotches · D stamp wave + dust puff.
Per-direction kill FX: A warm confetti shards · B implode + white shockwave · C white petals float up · D bouncy voxel debris.
Per-direction respawn FX: A sunrise halo + light beam · B glitch-in scanline · C ink-drop bloom · D rises from below + dust.
Side notifications: 4 distinct visual styles. Stack of 4, 3-second auto-dismiss, faction-tinted left border.
Faction-state banners: ENDANGERED / ELIMINATED / RECOVERED — center-screen, 4 distinct treatments (warm, neon-stripe, scroll, stamp).
Countdown: top-left widget per direction, with intensified styling for the last minute.
Game win FX: A 3D fireworks · B laser-grid sweep + ring pulse · C arena-wide watercolor wash · D voxel rain.
Distinct character + trail + minimap treatment per direction — see the FX matrix table below.

Full rationale + per-feature design tables live in docs/visual-direction-plan.md.

Reference — Current State

Current art direction: white background, flat lighting, no VFX, current faction palette.

What's wrong today

  • Kill / death = mesh disappears. No FX, no shake. Reads as a bug.
  • Claim = silent. Texture updates with no flash, no animation.
  • White everywhere. No atmosphere.
  • Trail vs same-faction territory disappears.
  • No notifications. Player can't tell when they killed, captured, or died.
  • No faction-state announcements. Endangered / eliminated transitions are silent.
  • No game-win moment. Static end-screen modal.

Why a hybrid? The Director's previous Slice A (E shipped) had two visible breakages: (1) the island geometry overlapped the territory plane at the same Y, causing Z-fighting "blinking", and (2) water waves crossed onto the playable area. F resolves both by structural means: a single flat cylinder sets the island top to a fixed height, the territory plane sits 0.05u above it, and the water shader discards everything inside `ARENA_RADIUS * 1.04`. The kill/respawn/banner/countdown/win moments come from D (Voxel Plate) because the box-debris language reinforces the chunky island silhouette better than E's softer splash particles. Notifications come from A (cream cards) because the warm legibility wins over driftwood at small sizes — and the Director explicitly anchored them below the leaderboard so they read as a feed, not a stack.

The dashed rectangles in the panel (FACTIONS / LEADERBOARD / MINIMAP / PLAYER STATS) are mock HUD anchors to verify the notifications-below-leaderboard rule visually. They are not real UI, just spatial guides.

Jen's recommendation (round 3)

Ship Tier 0 (#1–#5) immediately, regardless of direction. They are universally needed and direction-neutral.

Primary direction: D (Voxel Plate). Maximum expression of the box constraint. Lowest perf (+0.3ms), lowest risk. The voxel-debris kill FX, the build-up respawn, and the voxel-rain win all reinforce the tabletop identity. The wooden-tag notification is the standout — it makes every event feel like a label clipped onto the diorama.

Strongest "world": E (Castaway Atoll). Round 3's new direction is the only one that answers "what is this arena sitting on" with a real environment instead of an abstract surface. The animated water gives gameplay a sense of place; the foam-ring kill FX, wash-ashore respawn, and rising-flag win all sell the castaway fantasy. Trade-off: needs a few more days of art tuning (cliff rocks, distant atolls, cap variants) and a faction-palette retune to keep Blue clear of the sea hue. If the Director wants the game to read as a place at a glance, E is the pick.

If energy needed: B (Neon Pulse). Sharpest, most "alive" FX of the four. The implode-kill and laser-grid win are the most dramatic. Trade-off is yellow/green palette overlap.

Most artistically distinctive: C (Painted Plains). The petals-kill, ink-bleed claim, and watercolor-wash win are the most recognizable from a screenshot. Highest tuning cost.

Safe arcade default: A (Sunset Arcade). If D feels too "twee" or B too aggressive, A is the friendly arcade middle ground.
